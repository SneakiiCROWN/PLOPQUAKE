# Third-party assets and release notes

## Audio included in this project

Creator: https://nathangibson.myportfolio.com
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Files from original pack: OGG/Minimalist4.ogg (ui_tap), OGG/Modern2.ogg (ui_open), OGG/Minimalist2.ogg (ui_back), OGG/Modern9.ogg (ui_error), OGG/Minimalist7.ogg (ui_switch), OGG/Retro7.ogg (ui_confirm). Names changed solely to document in-game purposes; audio otherwise unmodified.

**Kenney Interface Sounds** by **Kenney**, licensed **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

**Kenney Music Jingles** by **Kenney**, licensed **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles

**Kenney Impact Sounds** by **Kenney**, licensed **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Available for blob collisions, landings, drops, and BOOM effects.

Source: https://wenrexa.itch.io/darkmiko
Included: two green action-panel images, used as nine-patch backgrounds for large, green interactive controls. Attribution optional under CC0, provided here for transparency.

**Kenney UI Pack** by **Kenney**, licensed **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**.

The supplied UI Icons pack by jfranci_px has an explicit commercial-use licence, but its 16px pixel-art aesthetics did not fit this build. No files from it are included. Other third-party packs in this conversation are not bundled pending independent confirmation of their commercial redistribution terms and visual suitability.

The pre-existing PLOPQUAKE artwork and code are preserved from the owner's supplied native project, with public prerelease distribution authorized by the owner. The third-party components above retain their own licences; no blanket asset-reuse licence is granted.
